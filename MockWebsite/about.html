Welcome to Projex Renovations

Our Story

Projex Renovations was founded in 2009 by two seasoned contractors who shared a simple belief: every homeowner deserves big-company quality with small-company personal service. What started as a two-person crew has grown into a full-service renovation company with a team of skilled renovations experts.

Over the past 15 years, we've completed hundreds of residential projects across Southwestern Ontario. From single-room refreshes and kitchen remodels to complete home renovations and new additions. What you see here is only the tip of the iceberg. Our repeat client rate and referral-driven growth speak to the trust we've built with homeowners throughout the region.

Our Values

We believe a great work starts long before the first nail is driven. It starts with listening. We take the time to understand your goals, your budget, and your family's needs before a single tool is lifted. At Projex, transparent pricing, honest timelines, and clean job sites are the standard.

Quality workmanship is where we stand out from the rest. At Projex we pride ourselves on quality and we ensure that the work we do leaves you smiling and only calling back to ask "What else can you do?"
